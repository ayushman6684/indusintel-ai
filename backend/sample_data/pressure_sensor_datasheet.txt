PRESSURESENSE CORP
Technical Datasheet

Product: PressureSense PS-200 Industrial Pressure Sensor
Product Code: PS-200
Category: Sensors
Manufacturer: PressureSense Corp

Description:
High-accuracy piezoresistive pressure sensor for industrial process
monitoring, HVAC systems, and hydraulic equipment.

Technical Specifications:
- Material: Stainless Steel 316L wetted parts
- Dimensions: Length 65 mm, Width 22 mm, Height 22 mm
- Weight: 0.15 kg
- Voltage: 24V DC (10-30V DC range)
- Power: 0.5 W
- Pressure rating: 0 to 25 bar
- Accuracy: +/- 0.25% full scale
- Operating temperature range: -40C to 125C
- Output signal: 4-20mA

Certifications: CE, RoHS, IP67

Applications:
- Hydraulic system monitoring
- HVAC pressure control
- Industrial process automation
- Compressor monitoring

Features:
- Robust stainless steel housing
- High EMC immunity
- Fast response time (<5ms)
- Wide temperature compensation range
